How it works

Three steps, all in your browser. You decide when recording starts and stops.

  1. 1

    Start tracking

    Pick how often to save your position: every 1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes. Each point is stored on your phone first, with its time and accuracy.

  2. 2

    Back it up privately Coming soon

    Sign in and register your browser to back up your history to your account. Only you can see it. There are no public or shareable location links.

  3. 3

    Get help when it matters Coming soon

    Press SOS to alert your trusted contacts, or start a trip and Safe Location asks if you're OK when you're late or your updates stop.

What Safe Location will never do

Safety tools hold sensitive data. These limits are built into how it works.

  • Track anyone without their consent. Only the person holding the phone can start recording, and the browser asks for permission first.
  • Make your location public. No public pages, no shareable links. Your history is visible only to you when signed in.
  • Claim a message was delivered when it wasn't. "Delivered" appears only after the SMS or email provider confirms it.
  • Say you're in danger. Messages describe what is known, like a missed check-in, and nothing more.
  • Pretend to be an emergency service. It alerts your contacts. For the police, fire or ambulance, call 112.
  • Promise what a browser can't do. Recording runs while the tracker is open. If you close it or your phone switches off, recording stops.
