Layout A: map first, everything one swipe away

A clickable prototype of the simplified Safe Location tracker. Everything runs in this page with simulated data: no real location, no real messages. One minute of tracking passes in 5 seconds.

Things to try

  1. Start tracking, then drag the sheet up and down
  2. Start a trip to Lekki with trusted contacts
  3. Tap SOS: a 5-second countdown you can cancel
  4. Hold the logo (top left) for 3 seconds: silent SOS
  5. Cancel an SOS with the safety PIN, or the duress PIN
